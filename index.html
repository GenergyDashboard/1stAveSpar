Environmental Impact Calculations

Our impact metrics are based on:

  • Trees: 1 tree absorbs ~21kg CO₂/year. We calculate equivalent trees needed to offset your solar generation.
  • Homes: Average SA home uses ~900 kWh/month. Your solar power could run this many homes.
  • Coal: ~1kg coal = 1 kWh electricity. Your solar directly replaces this coal consumption.
  • Water: Coal power uses ~2L water per kWh. Solar saves all this water.
